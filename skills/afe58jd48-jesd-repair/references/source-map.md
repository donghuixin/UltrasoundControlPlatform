# Local source map and applicability

The original documents may be selective-disclosure or NDA material. Keep them local; do not copy their full text, figures, register tables, or RTL into a public repository.

## Directly useful

- `AFE58JD48EVM_User_Guide__SLOU521_.pdf`: official EVM initialization and 120 MSPS/eight-lane quick-start context. Its capture-board screenshots and connector examples target a different TSW board in places, so do not copy connector numbers to TSW14J50.
- `sbas881a.pdf`: local AFE58JD48 device reference for JESD mode/readback and digital test capabilities. A second file named `sbas881a (1).pdf` is byte-identical and adds no evidence.
- `JESD204B_IP_Configuration.pdf` plus local instantiation RTL: explains the architecture of a TI/Xilinx reference receiver and its separate lane decoder. It is not the TSW14J50RX firmware source.
- `MHR054A_Sch.PDF`: useful for TX7316EVM net names and trigger/T/R connector verification, but contains no AFE-to-TSW JESD deformatter implementation.

## Important applicability limits

- The JESD reference design was validated in a different transmit mode and with only a subset of lanes active. Its example defaults must not be copied into this 120 MSPS, PLL40x, eight-physical-lane system.
- For this target, two converters per physical lane and all eight physical lanes are expected at the aggregate system level. Any custom FPGA decoder must be audited accordingly.
- The AFE is two 8-channel links/dies. Per-die readbacks and aggregate HSDC values are not interchangeable.
- The reference RTL shows that successful link output can still be misassembled by a downstream lane/converter decoder. It does not prove that HSDC Pro uses the same implementation.
- Public/local PDFs do not define the private HSDC meanings of `JESD IP Core_M`, `Channel Pattern`, `Group128`, or the exact TSW14J50RX converter lookup table. Do not guess those encodings after controlled tests show no effect.

## Not useful for this digital fault

- TX7316 documents: useful for transmit safety, waveform, T/R switch, and trigger behavior, but they cannot explain duplication generated by internal AFE converter codes.
- Field II guide: useful for acoustic field and array simulation only.
- `sszz027n.pdf`, `sszz027o.pdf`, and `slvt174.pdf`: EVM terms and high-voltage safety only; no channel-deformatter evidence.
